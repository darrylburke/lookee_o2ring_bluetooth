All full nights

Medians, not single nights: oxygen-drop counts vary by about ±4 per hour from one night to the next, and one night misclassifies severity 20–50 % of the time; accuracy levels off at about 14 nights. A consumer ring is accurate to about ±2–3 % SpO2. Nothing here is a diagnosis.
